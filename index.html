About me

Hi, I’m Yashaswini.

I’m a software engineer with a B.Tech in Computer Science from Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham. My work centres on backend engineering: Java and Spring Boot REST APIs, Kafka event-driven processing, and data-heavy services on PostgreSQL and Elasticsearch.

I like the unglamorous part of reliability: reading logs and stack traces until the root cause is clear, then writing tests so the fix stays fixed. I enjoy owning a piece of work from requirements through release, and I’m looking to grow into a product-minded engineer who understands the business problem behind the code.

I also build React front ends and agentic AI prototypes, and I keep learning through problem solving, with 250+ DSA problems solved and a published IEEE I2CT 2024 paper on steganography.
